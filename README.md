\# 🚗 Traffic Dodge



\### Dodge the traffic. Survive the road. Beat your high score!



Traffic Dodge is a 2D arcade traffic-dodging game developed using \*\*Godot Engine 4\*\* and \*\*GDScript\*\*.



Take control of your car, avoid incoming vehicles, collect points by passing traffic, and test your survival skills as the difficulty increases!



\## 🎮 Gameplay



Your goal is simple: avoid incoming enemy cars and survive for as long as possible.



Every vehicle you successfully pass earns you points. But be careful! As your score increases, enemy cars become faster, making the challenge more difficult.



You start with three lives. How long can you survive?



\## ✨ Game Features



\- 🚗 Player-controlled car movement

\- 🚙 Five different enemy car designs

\- ❤️ Three-life health system

\- 🏆 Score tracking system

\- 📈 Three difficulty stages

\- ⚡ Increasing enemy vehicle speed

\- 🌃 Neon-inspired city and road visuals

\- 🎵 Background music and sound effects

\- 📖 Instructions screen

\- 🔄 Play Again and Main Menu options

\- 🏁 Victory and Game Over screens



\## 🕹️ Controls



| Key | Action |

|---|---|

| ⬅️ Left Arrow / A | Move Left |

| ➡️ Right Arrow / D | Move Right |



\## 📊 Difficulty System



The game features three difficulty stages:



| Level | Score |

|---|---|

| Level 1 | Below 250 |

| Level 2 | 250–499 |

| Level 3 | 500 and above |



As you progress, enemy vehicles move faster and avoiding traffic becomes more challenging.



\## 🏆 Scoring System



\- Earn \*\*10 points\*\* for each enemy car successfully passed.

\- Avoid collisions to preserve your lives.

\- Try to achieve the highest possible score!
